ABOUT

minesweeeper.xyz is a small, free Minesweeper game that runs in your browser. It is built to feel like a classic retro game: two colors, pixel-style icons, and simple controls that work well on a phone.

What makes this version different

  • A two-color look inspired by classic offline browser games.
  • Touch controls designed for phones: tap to dig, press and hold to plant a flag.
  • Three difficulty levels with square boards that fit your screen.
  • Short animations for digging, planting flags, restarting, and clearing a board.

Why we made it

Minesweeper is one of the best-known logic puzzles, but many versions are cluttered or hard to use on a small screen. We wanted a clean version that opens instantly, needs no download or account, and still feels satisfying to play.

Keeping the game free

The game is free. To cover hosting and development, the site may show advertising. You can read how advertising and cookies are handled in our Privacy Policy.
